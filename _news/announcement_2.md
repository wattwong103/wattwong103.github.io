From April 2024 to April 2025 I worked as a researcher in the Academic Division of the Pheu Thai Party, designing data-driven public-transport policy from the 2022 Household Travel Survey (Ministry of Transport).

The work covered congestion-charge planning for the Bangkok Metropolitan Area, bus routes and mode-shift incentives in Lat Krabang, and the data infrastructure behind those policies. It was a year of turning survey evidence into routes, incentives, and briefing notes — not a current role.

Key areas of focus:

- Analyzing transportation data to inform policy decisions
- Designing bus routes and incentives for public transport mode shifting
- Building data and policy infrastructure from government datasets
