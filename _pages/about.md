I am a graduate student at the [Center for Spatial Information Science (CSIS)](https://www.csis.u-tokyo.ac.jp/), University of Tokyo. My research lies at the intersection of transportation engineering, urban planning, and machine learning.

I graduated with first-class honors in Civil Engineering from [Chulalongkorn University](https://www.chula.ac.th/en/), where I developed a strong foundation in transportation modeling, travel behavior analysis, and discrete choice methods. During my undergraduate years, I worked as a Research Associate at the [Chulalongkorn University Transportation Institute (CUTI)](http://www.cuti.chula.ac.th/), contributing to projects in mode choice modeling, transportation planning, and traffic engineering.

Previously, I served as a Researcher at the [ARIISE Research Unit](https://ariise.eng.chula.ac.th/), focusing on railway infrastructure, flood risk mapping, and machine learning-based prognostics for track buckling. I also worked with the Pheu Thai Party's Academic Division, analyzing household travel survey data and designing bus route policies for Bangkok.

My research interests include:
- **Transportation modeling**: mode choice, travel demand forecasting, and discrete choice models
- **Machine learning for infrastructure**: predictive maintenance, flood risk assessment, and spatial analysis
- **Sustainable urban mobility**: transit-oriented development, public transportation policy, and open data platforms

I am passionate about using data-driven approaches to make transportation systems more efficient, equitable, and sustainable.
