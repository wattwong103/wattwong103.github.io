I teach as a lecturer and faculty advisor at [Mahidol Wittayanusorn School](https://www.mwit.ac.th/) (MWIT) in Nakhon Pathom, since July 2020. The work covers the National Geography Olympiad, plus support for the Social Science department: classwork, exam reading and fieldwork. This is school teaching, not a university course list. I do not currently lecture at Chulalongkorn or the University of Tokyo.

MWIT is also where I went to school. I studied in its STEM programme from 2016 to 2019, won a bronze medal at the International Geography Olympiad in 2018, and did a school project using GIS to survey flood-risk areas in Nakhon Pathom. I now teach the same competition I trained for.

The lesson files are not online. If you want the Olympiad syllabus or fieldwork notes, [email me](mailto:w.wongkaew@csis.u-tokyo.ac.jp).

## Geography Olympiad

I created the syllabus and curriculum for National Geography Olympiad training. It includes:

- Lesson plans covering physical and human geography
- Fieldwork guides for geographic data collection and analysis
- Practice exams drawn from past Olympiad papers
- Exercises in spatial thinking and map interpretation

## Fieldwork and human geography

Training modules used with Olympiad students: research methods in the field, plus urbanisation, migration and environmental management, usually through Thai case studies.

## Volunteer tutoring

Volunteer academic tutor at the Police Border Patrol School in Chumphon, a remote school in southern Thailand.
