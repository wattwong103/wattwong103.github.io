I teach as a lecturer and faculty advisor at [Mahidol Wittayanusorn School](https://www.mwit.ac.th/) (2020–present): the National Geography Olympiad, and support for the Social Science department (classwork, exam reading, fieldwork). This is school teaching, not a university course list. I do not currently lecture at Chulalongkorn or the University of Tokyo.

I do not have the lesson files online. If you want the Olympiad syllabus or fieldwork notes, [email me](mailto:w.wongkaew@csis.u-tokyo.ac.jp).

## Geography Olympiad

- Lesson plans covering physical and human geography
- Fieldwork guides for geographic data collection and analysis
- Practice exams drawn from past Olympiad papers
- Exercises in spatial thinking and map interpretation

## Fieldwork and human geography

Training modules used with Olympiad students: research methods in the field, plus urbanization, migration, and environmental management, usually through Thai case studies.
