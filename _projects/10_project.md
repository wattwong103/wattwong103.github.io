Investigator on the Generative Urban Foundation Model (GUFM) in Sekimoto Lab (IIS, CSIS), The University of Tokyo — generating realistic human-mobility trajectories across global cities from real data with a decoder-only transformer, and exploring world models and diffusion methods.

This is current research (2025–present). The academic write-up lives on the [research one-pager](https://wattwong103.github.io/research/).
